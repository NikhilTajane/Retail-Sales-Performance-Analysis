# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## About the Project

This is a retail sales analysis project that I created to practice and apply my data analysis skills using Python.

The main goal of this project was to analyze retail transaction data and find useful business insights related to revenue, product categories, sales channels, cities, customer acquisition, and discounts.

I focused not only on creating charts but also on understanding what the data means from a business point of view.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

- Analyze revenue trends over time
- Identify the highest-performing product categories
- Understand revenue contribution by sales channel
- Identify top-performing cities
- Analyze customer acquisition channels
- Check whether discount percentage shows any relationship with revenue
- Convert the analysis into useful business insights and recommendations

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis

I created different visualizations to understand the sales performance.

### 1. Revenue Trend

I analyzed monthly revenue to understand how sales changed over time.

**Insight:**  
Revenue shows an overall upward trend, with stronger growth toward the later months of the analysis period.

**Business implication:**  
The business appears to have positive sales momentum, so it would be useful to investigate the factors driving the recent growth.

---

### 2. Revenue by Product Category

I compared revenue generated by different product categories.

**Insight:**  
Equipment is the highest revenue-generating category, with a clear gap compared with the lower-performing categories.

**Business implication:**  
The company can investigate what is driving the strong performance of Equipment and identify opportunities to improve weaker categories.

---

### 3. Revenue by Sales Channel

I analyzed revenue contribution from different sales channels.

**Key finding:**

**Website + Mobile App = 74% of total revenue**

**Business implication:**  
Digital channels are major contributors to revenue. Improving the digital customer journey and understanding lower-performing channels could help improve overall performance.

---

### 4. Top Cities by Revenue

I analyzed revenue across cities to identify the strongest geographic markets.

**Insight:**  
Chennai generates the highest revenue among the analyzed cities.

**Business implication:**  
High-performing cities could be prioritized for expansion, while lower-performing cities can be investigated for growth opportunities.

---

### 5. Customer Acquisition Channel

I analyzed revenue based on the channel through which customers were acquired.

**Insight:**  
Website-acquired customers generate the highest total revenue, followed by Mobile App acquisition.

**Business implication:**  
It would be useful to understand which acquisition campaigns and customer behaviors are contributing to the strong performance of digital acquisition channels.

---

### 6. Discount Percentage vs Revenue

I also analyzed the relationship between discount percentage and revenue.

**Insight:**  
Revenue is distributed across different discount levels, and there is no clear upward or downward relationship visible in the analysis.

**Business implication:**  
Discounting alone may not be a strong driver of revenue. Further analysis would be required before making changes to the discount strategy.

---

## Key Business Insights

From the analysis, I found the following:

1. Revenue shows positive growth over time.
2. Equipment is the strongest revenue-generating category.
3. Website and Mobile App contribute the majority of channel revenue.
4. Revenue is concentrated in a few strong-performing cities.
5. Website acquisition generates the highest total revenue.
6. Discount percentage does not show a clear relationship with revenue.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, I would recommend:

- Continue improving high-performing digital sales channels.
- Investigate the factors behind Equipment's strong performance.
- Develop strategies specific to high-performing and low-performing cities.
- Investigate lower-performing product categories and sales channels.
- Analyze customer-level behavior in more detail in future work.
- Perform deeper analysis of discounting before changing the discount strategy.
